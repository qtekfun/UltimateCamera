**Pre-release 0.8.16**.

- **White balance follows the lens**: in AUTO, zooming to another lens (for example 3x) kept the main lens's colour settings, so the picture turned grey, purple or green. The app now detects the lens switch and takes the colour settings of the new lens.
- **Settings › Diagnostic log**: optional and off by default. While it is on, the app writes what the camera does (lens in use, white balance, sessions, errors, auto-configuration results) to a file on the phone, to share for analysis. It costs some performance. No identifiers; nothing is sent unless you share it.
- **Preview clear of the controls**: the video is fitted into the part of the screen the controls leave free, so nothing of the frame sits under the REC column (landscape) or the bottom bar (portrait).

### What to test
1. Turn on Settings › Diagnostic log. In AUTO, switch between 1x, 3x and the other lenses: colours must stay natural. Then share the log.
2. In landscape, the right edge of the picture must be visible next to the REC column; tap to focus still lands where you tap.
