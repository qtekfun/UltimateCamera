**Pre-release 0.8.11**.

- **Smooth motion before bit depth**: when a camera holds 4K60 only in 8-bit, the app now records 4K60 8-bit instead of 4K30 10-bit. 4K60 10-bit still comes first when it holds.
- **The selfie camera is measured too**: auto-configuration tests the front camera after the main one. Results are grouped per camera, the front camera gets its own best mode, and switching to the selfie camera uses the modes it really holds instead of its untested list.
- **"No image"**: a mode where the camera delivers no frame at all now says so instead of "too slow".
- **Experimental: frame pacing at 4K60**: with Settings › Experimental on, auto-configuration also tries the video modes and recording hints the camera itself lists, to see if any of them paces 4K60 better. Results are shown only; they are not used for recording yet. Each probe also shows how many buffers the camera reported lost.
- Auto-configuration takes a couple of minutes and runs again on every phone.

### What to test
1. Run auto-configuration: there should be a "Front camera" section with its own best mode.
2. On a phone that held 4K60 only in 8-bit, the best mode should now be 4K60 8-bit.
3. With Experimental on, check which 4K60 variants hold and how many buffers they lose.
