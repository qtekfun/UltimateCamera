**Pre-release 0.8.6**.

- **Auto-configuration no longer depends on the light**: in low light a camera stretches a single frame now and then, which no app can avoid, and the probe counted it as a dropped frame, so 60 fps modes were rejected when the phone was pointed at a dark scene. Those frames are now counted apart; frames lost by the GPU or the encoder still reject a mode. Stored results are probed again on the next auto-configuration.

### What to test
1. Settings → Run auto-configuration, phone face down: 4K60 is held on a phone whose own camera app records 4K60.
