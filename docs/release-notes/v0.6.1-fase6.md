**Lenses** (pre-release, Phase 6).

### New since v0.6.0-fase6
- **Lens picker** read from the phone: 0.5× (ultra-wide), 1×, 2× (crop), tele where present, and the front camera; pinch to zoom; on a logical camera the lens changes during a take.
- **Front camera**: preview mirrored, recording not mirrored and upright.
- **Stabilisation** chip (electronic modes the camera offers, OIS on/off).
- **Second camera**: choose the pair among those the phone supports (Settings › Multicamera).
- **HDR viewfinder**: on HDR screens, 10-bit HLG takes are shown in HDR in the preview (Settings › Display). Experimental sensor HDR video where the phone exposes a vendor mode (Settings › Experimental, then auto-configuration).
- Proxy on 10-bit takes fixed; auto-configuration no longer reports false drops.

### What to test
1. The lens pills match your phone's real lenses (OPPO: ultra-wide, main, tele). Tap each while recording; check the framing changes and note any pause.
2. Front camera: preview like a mirror, file not mirrored (text reads correctly), upright in portrait and both landscapes.
3. Stabilisation chip: try each mode at 4K30 and 1080p; some may be refused and fall to the next mode.
4. HDR viewfinder: at 4K30 10-bit without LUT the highlights look brighter than with the setting off.
5. Diagnostics › Run everything › Export report, and send it (it lists the phone's lenses, HDR and vendor keys).
