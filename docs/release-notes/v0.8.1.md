**Pre-release 0.8.1**.

- **White balance (Auto)**: balanced on the whole scene instead of the camera's own estimate, which followed the dominant colour (a brown box filling the frame turned the white wall behind it blue). Bright, unclipped areas lead; the colour temperature moves smoothly, so it does not flicker while recording. AWB-L freezes it; presets and manual Kelvin are unchanged. Phones without manual white balance keep the camera's own.

### What to test
1. Auto white balance: a large brown or coloured object in front of a white wall; the wall should stay white.
2. Indoor warm light and daylight: Auto settles in about a second and holds steady during a take.
3. AWB-L on: the colour stays fixed when the framing changes.
