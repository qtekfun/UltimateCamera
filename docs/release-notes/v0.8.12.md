**Pre-release 0.8.12**.

- **The screen stays on during auto-configuration**: a run takes a couple of minutes without a touch, and the screen timeout used to stop it halfway.
- **4K on cameras that do not list it**: auto-configuration now also tries sizes up to 4K that the camera does not advertise, and 4K30 in 8-bit on cameras without 10-bit. A selfie camera that records 4K30 in the phone's own app but listed only 1080p can now get 4K30 too. A refusal costs about a second.
- Auto-configuration runs again on every phone.

### What to test
1. Start auto-configuration and leave the phone untouched: it should finish without the screen turning off.
2. On a phone whose own camera app records 4K selfies, the "Front camera" section should show 4K30 tried, and held if the camera delivers it.
