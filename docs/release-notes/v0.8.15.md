**Pre-release 0.8.15**.

- **Sharper selfie, and 4K selfie**: a camera that does not list 4K now records at its largest real 16:9 size (3264x1836 on an 8 MP selfie sensor) instead of 1080p. It can also record 4K upscaled from that size on the GPU, as the phone's own camera app does for its "4K" selfie. Upscaled modes are marked with ↑, and the auto-configuration results explain the mark.
- Auto-configuration runs again on every phone to measure the new modes.

### What to test
1. Run auto-configuration and check the "Front camera" section: the camera's real size and the ↑ 4K modes should appear with their results.
2. Switch to the selfie camera, pick the 4K ↑ mode and record: the file must be 3840x2160 and the image must not be stretched.
