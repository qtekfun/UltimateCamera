**Pre-release 0.8.0** (phases 6–9).

- **Auto-configuration**: a single dropped frame no longer throws away 4K60: a marginal result is measured again, and only clear failures imply that heavier modes fail. Each row shows the largest frame gap. Stored results are probed again once.
- **Photo look**: whites reach white and shadows are no longer crushed (local tone mapping without halos), on every path we render; Ultra HDR photos keep their HDR highlights.
- **Speed (Pixel 8, 12 MP)**: base rendering 0.6 s, HDR fusion about 2.2 s, colour style 0.4 s, portrait photo 1.6 s.
- **Colour styles**: restyle the last photo from its unstyled copy; choose the camera's styles from the monitor.
- **Portrait photo** (optional): GPU blur, live blurred preview, tap to choose what stays sharp (the lens focuses there too), change aperture and focus after the shot, better edges where depth is weak.
- **Portrait video** (optional, 8-bit up to 4K): background blur that steps down instead of dropping frames.
- **Separate WAV**: a slow card no longer loses sync; missing audio becomes silence in place.
- **Fixes**: portrait shots no longer crash; GPU HDR fusion matches the reference.

### What to test
1. OPPO: auto-configuration again → 4K60 rows probed and held.

3. Portrait on (Settings › Photo): tap to focus, shoot, open the last photo › Portrait tab, change the aperture.
4. Video with Blur on (Settings › Recording): a 1-minute take keeps its frame rate.
