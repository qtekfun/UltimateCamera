**Pre-release 0.8.14**.

- **Camera diagnostics in Settings**: a new "Camera diagnostics" section lists what every camera the app can see offers for video. It covers listed cameras, the physical cameras behind them and ids that answer although they are not listed. For each one it shows output sizes per format, the maximum-resolution sizes, 10-bit profiles and vendor size tables. The report is read-only, opens no camera and contains no device identifier. Nothing is sent unless you copy or share it.

### What to test
1. Settings › Camera diagnostics › Generate report.
2. Check that the front camera and its physical cameras appear, then copy or share the text.
