**Pre-release 0.8.10**.

- **Occasional dropped frames no longer cost a mode**: some phones' cameras leave a short gap now and then at 4K60, in their own camera apps too. Auto-configuration used to judge that from a 4-second sample and fall back to 4K30. Now a mode that loses a few frames in short gaps is measured again for 15 seconds: up to 1 % lost and no gap over 100 ms counts as "holds (drops a frame now and then)" and can be used. More than that still fails as before. Auto-configuration can take one or two minutes and runs again on every phone.
- **Manufacturer keys line**: shows one count per key group. The second figure was always equal to the first and said nothing.

### What to test
1. Run auto-configuration on a phone that dropped to 4K30 before: 4K60 should now hold, or show "holds (drops a frame now and then)".
