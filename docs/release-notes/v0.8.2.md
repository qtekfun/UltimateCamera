**Pre-release 0.8.2**.

- **White balance (Auto)**: fixes 0.8.1, which turned grey walls cyan on some phones. The correction now adjusts red and blue directly (tint included) and looks for the colour cast shared by the most bright surfaces, so a lit orange or brown object is not taken for the light. It stays within what the sensor can need under real light, even when the phone's own estimate is wrong. Phones whose camera cannot take manual white-balance gains are corrected on the GPU. AWB-L freezes it; presets and manual Kelvin are unchanged.

### What to test
1. A grey or white wall with a large orange or brown object in front: the wall stays grey, the object keeps its colour.
2. Warm indoor light and daylight: Auto settles in about two seconds and holds steady during a take.
3. AWB-L on: the colour stays fixed when the framing changes.
